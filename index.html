About us

PetRock Capital is a venture firm that invests in breakthrough blockchain technologies, ranging from ground-breaking Web 3.0 infrastructure to degenerately unorthodox concepts.

We adopt an active investment approach and we firmly believe in delivering concrete value to the projects we invest in.

Our vision is to build Asia Pacific's leading blockchain fund that maximises value creation.

Our story

From very early on we had conviction in this space, while the majority of people were still very skeptical and dismissed cryptocurrencies as a fraud or a useless rock.

When Mark Cuban famously stated he would take a Pet Rock over Bitcoin, inspiration struck. We solidified our conviction amidst widespread skepticism.

Today, Bitcoin is among the greatest performing asset class the world has ever seen. The name Pet Rock serves as a reminder that it is the unconventional ideas that go on to rock the world.
